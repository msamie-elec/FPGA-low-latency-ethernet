**Project:** FPGA Low-Latency Ethernet Processing - Dual Artix-7 Hardware Demonstrator

Designed a SystemVerilog Ethernet/UDP processing platform on two Digilent Arty A7-35T FPGAs, featuring a fixed-format synthetic market-data processor, asynchronous RX/TX FIFO buffering and an independent hardware-paced traffic generator. Hardware validation showed approximately 12.85 us median FPGA-to-FPGA round-trip time for the buffered endpoint and 9,000/9,000 correct replies across nine finite bursts at FPGA-controlled request rates of 10k, 50k and 100k packets/s. Built testbenches, Vivado timing/CDC audits and reproducible Python hardware tests. These are experimental board-level results, not production throughput guarantees.
